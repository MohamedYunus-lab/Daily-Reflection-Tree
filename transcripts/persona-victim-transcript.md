## Analysis

This persona demonstrated:
- **External locus:** Felt stuck by circumstances, saw little personal influence, attributed outcomes to external factors
- **Entitlement orientation:** Frustrated by lack of acknowledgment, wanted more recognition, compared self unfavorably to others who "aren't pulling their weight"
- **Self-centric radius:** Focused on own problem, worried about own performance and evaluation

The tree correctly identified this as the "victim/entitled/self-centric" path. Critically, the summary doesn't shame or judge—it names the pattern ("That's a hard place to be") and points to a concrete path forward ("find one small thing you controlled"). This maintains the tree's core philosophy: awareness without judgment, direction without demand.

The reflections throughout were compassionate:
- "That's natural" (validating the difficulty)
- "That's not wrong—fairness matters" (acknowledging legitimate concerns)
- "That's human" (normalizing self-focus)

But each reflection also plants a seed:
- "Even deciding to wait is a choice" (agency exists even in constraint)
- "Is the scorecard the point?" (questioning the entitlement frame)
- "Problems don't disappear—they just take up less of the sky" (showing the benefit of widening perspective)

This is the tree working as designed: meeting the employee where they are, then gently pointing toward growth.
